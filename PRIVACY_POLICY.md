# Privacy Policy for PitchSwara

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2026  
**Application Name:** PitchSwara (`com.singeek.pitchswara`)  
**Developer:** Singeek  

## 1. Overview
**PitchSwara** is a standalone vocal pitch monitor and singing practice tool for Android. Your privacy is critically important to us. PitchSwara is designed to operate **100% offline** on your device and **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or audio recordings**.

## 2. Microphone Permission (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`)
PitchSwara requests access to your device's microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) solely to provide its core functionality:
- **Real-Time Pitch Detection:** Microphone audio is analyzed locally in real time in your device's memory (RAM) to calculate and display your vocal pitch frequency (Hz) and musical note on the graph.
- **Temporary Vocal Feedback (`Rec / Play`):** When you use the optional **Rec**, **Stop**, and **Play** buttons at the bottom of the screen, audio is held strictly in temporary volatile memory (RAM) so you can immediately listen back to your singing. It is **never saved as a file on your device storage**, is immediately overwritten when you start a new recording, and is permanently cleared as soon as the app closes.

## 3. Data Collection, Storage, and Sharing
- **No Personal Information Collected:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.
- **No Internet Transmission:** PitchSwara does not request `INTERNET` permission and does not connect to any external servers. Your voice and audio never leave your device.
- **No Third-Party SDKs or Ads:** PitchSwara contains no third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking libraries.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because PitchSwara does not collect or transmit any personal information from any user of any age, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA).
